The chapter's snipe app. Spot a brother on campus, snap them from a distance, post it. Takes 2 minutes to set up.

Open this page on your phone

Open this in Safari first. You're in another app's browser, which can't add apps to your Home Screen. Tap ⋯ or the share icon and choose Open in Safari.
  1. Tap the gold button below to open the app in Safari.Don't sign in there yet.
  2. Tap Share, scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.On some iPhones Share is under the ⋯ button.
  3. Close Safari and open See a Send a from your Home Screen.
  4. Enter your email and the chapter code from the card.Didn't get the code? Ask a brother.
  5. Type the 8-digit code from your email.It comes from martinventuresdev@gmail.com (sender name "See a Send a"). Check Junk if it's not there in a minute.
  6. Set your name, then go to Settings and turn on Snipe alerts.
